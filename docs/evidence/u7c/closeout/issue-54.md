### U7C: hard DEV retention stop prevented an unchecked 256-update extension (11 October 2026)

U7C began from the accepted U6 learner at optimizer step 13,400, preserving trained weights and AdamW moments. Its counterbalanced grammar passed pregradient shortcut tests; training kept the exact 12/4/8/8 allocation. The deterministic conservation policy was frozen before gradient updates and checked every 16 steps. At 16 updates all five critical gates passed (Boolean primitives 26/26, U4 historical composite 46/128, U4 varied 89/128, U0 composite 71/128, lineage 5/10). At 32 updates, U4 historical composite fell to 39/128 below the 42/128 floor; lineage fell to 3/10 below 4/10. Primitives remained 26/26, U4 varied 85/128 and U0 composite 69/128. The optimizer stopped at 13,432 after 32 of the 256 allowed updates.

Fresh full-stage final accuracy changed only 226/448 to 231/448, and balanced cross-stage final 161/288 to 162/288. Pre-frozen matched three-case composition correctness remained 9/144 to 10/144. This is a measured negative retention/composition outcome; an early stop is not a successful stage promotion and does not imply absence of more gradual forgetting.

For #54, preserve independent critical Boolean, U4 evidence and lineage fingerprints in every future learning epoch, with explicit tolerated declines and a mandatory early stop before more gradients. Broad aggregate improvement must not override a failing local conservation gate. Keep an immutable baseline and an exact optimizer checkpoint so future experiments can compare interventions without treating this failed child as the new accepted parent. Teacher English is not natural-language acquisition or language-specific forgetting evidence.
