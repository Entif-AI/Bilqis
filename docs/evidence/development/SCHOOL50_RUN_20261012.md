## What the teacher automatically did

For every current grade, a versioned curriculum provides earlier skill
prerequisites, backward spiral references and forward preview of the
next two grades. The teacher fills 32-example training batches with real
semantics, including previous and future lessons, never fake labels.
The independent typed finite-world/mathematical oracles remain responsible
for gold answers.

At every grade visit the teacher chooses a predefined policy vector:
rehearsal share, future-preview share, semantic contrast, question depth,
learning rate and distillation strength. It measures changing practical
performance and old-skill health on generated developmental questions,
stores per-grade and aggregate rewards, and periodically mutates **one
permitted variable** into a new strategy candidate. Its source code and
policy history are separate from the student's trained weights.

These probes are small and reused for coaching. Accuracy can go up or
down; there is **no sealed-generalization claim** and no assertion that
the teacher has learned a globally optimal curriculum. The operating
achievement is eliminating repeated manual intervention, preserving one
trained neural learner and autonomously selecting its lessons.

## Current scope limits

- **Executable actual curricula:** G01-G22 logic and mathematics, with
  repeated rising-grade spirals already demonstrated.
- **Designed but not executable:** G23-G35 New Ithkuil grammar and
  G36-G50 New Ithkuil lexicon. The full 50-grade pass has therefore
  NOT occurred. Unsupported grade generators fail explicit admission
  and are not pretended to be learned.
- Existing output is classification into five logical/evidence states.
  The present model context remains 96 tokens, and there is no real
  New Ithkuil sequence production head or curated root/stem semantic
  lexicon available to this trainer.
- Official 2023 New Ithkuil lexicon editions and rights need source
  resolution; an old 512-slot reserved token ABI is NOT a 6000-root
  linguistic ontology. No unlicensed donor corpus is committed here.
- Widening the neural model preserves function at the growth boundary,
  not future forgetting immunity. The user deliberately accepts local
  regression while seeking long-term compositional competence.
